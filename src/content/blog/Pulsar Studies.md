## Pulsar Studies
This work is inspired by *The New Pulsar Generator (nuPG)* by Marcin Pietruszewski and the pulsar synthesis concepts developed by Curtis Roads, particularly in *OPulWM*. While implementing the core principles of pulsar synthesis, I developed my own modifications to the synthesis design in C++(JUCE), most notably a multiple waveform scanning approach that is not found in nuPG. This produces variations in the resulting sound.
